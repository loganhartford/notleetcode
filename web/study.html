How to use this

Open a question to see your latest code. Use Start over to solve it from memory. Your edits save to the project automatically. Run the tests, explain your reasoning aloud, then rate yourself here.

Your ratings update Due for recall. The daily route is a suggested plan; it does not rewrite itself. For coaching, ask Codex to review your saved code and mistake notes and adjust the plan. There is no automatic AI review or recording of spoken answers.
